## DevOps core concept: CI/CD
### Continuous integration
- The practice of automatically building and unit testing an entire application frequently, ideally on every source code check-in.
### Continuous deployment (delivery)
- Additional step. The practice of automatically deploying every build to production after it passes its automated tests.

## Benefits of Continuous delivery
### Empowering Teams
- makes process transparrent for every team member
### Lowered Cycle Times
- can reduce delivery to hours or even minutes
### Better Security
- companies who continuously integrate security in their projects spend 50% less time remediating security issues
### Rhytm of Practice
- removes stres from teams both in development and operations
### More Time to be Productive
- it's a way to deliver value faster
- organisations and teams doing continuously delivery find they spend less time doing unplanned work or rework and more time adding new features
